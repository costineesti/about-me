[Source](https://pytorch.org/hub/intelisl_midas_v2/)

It is a modern solution brought to computer vision in 2020 for monocular cases where you only have a RGB input, nothing more and nothing less. I used it in my [personal project - jupyter notebook](https://github.com/costineesti/ComputerVision/blob/master/3_normal_picking_angle/normal_picking_angle.ipynb) where I had to compute the optimal surface for picking up packages.

>[!quote] ==MiDaS== computes relative inverse depth from a single image
