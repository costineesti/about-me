**What are the 2 core properties of relational representations?**

==Compositionality== = no tailor-made representations of R(X,Y) for specific X and Y (first time you see it).
==Systematicity== = If you understand R(X,Y), you understand it for arbitrary X and Y. E.g., also for
R(Y,X), even if not true or ‘nonsense’. So something like commutativity (if you understand `John fancies Mary`, then you also understand `Mary fancies John`).
