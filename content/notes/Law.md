You are asked to implement either EU's trustworthiness principles or the concept of meaningful human control. To do so, please follow the workflow below

1. **Option 1**: Apply EU's ALTAI tool to analyse the chosen system ([https://altai.insight-centre.org/](https://altai.insight-centre.org/)).
2. **Option 2**: Alternatively, develop a set of design principles that ensure Meaningful Human Control (MHC) in your system ([Robins 2023](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43681-023-00320-6); [Verbruggen 2019Links to an external site.](https://www.nonproliferation.eu/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/EUNPDC_no-65_031219.pdf "Link")).

ALTAI stands for Assessment List for Trustworthy AI. There are 7 requirements that need to be met in order to be trustworthy:

1. **Human Agency and Oversight**: fundamental rights, human agency and human oversight.
	1. There should always be a human in control, that can oversee the robot and interfere if necessary.
2. **Technical Robustness and Safety**: resilience to attack and security, fall back plan and general safety, accuracy, reliability and reproducibility.
	1. Security and Encryption.
	2. Thorough Testing
	3. Safe guards and fallback modes in case of error.
	4. Fly higher to avoid flying projectiles from the crowd.
3. **Privacy and Data Governance**: respect for privacy, quality and integrity of data, access to data.
	1. Privacy of people - blurring faces.
4. **Transparency**: traceability, explainability, communication.
	1. Spread awareness about the robot to the masses so they don't fear it when they see it for the first time. Make information available both on ground and online. This could avoid making people feel like they are being watched.
5. **Diversity, Non-discrimination and Fairness**: avoidance of unfair bias, accessibility and universal design.
	1. Avoid making biased decisions (tall, short, wheelchair, black, white, etc.)
6. **Societal and Environmental Well-being**: sustainability and environmental friendliness, social impact, society and democracy.
	1. Only assist medical and security, not replace them.
7. **Accountability**: auditability, minimization and reporting of negative impact, trade-offs and redress.
	1. The operator will be accountable for the behaviour of the robot. Preliminary Design should take care of most inconveniences that might appear in normal operation or at start/shut down. Draw a line where the operator or the engineering team is held accountable in case of malfunction.
