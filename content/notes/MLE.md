In statistics, maximul likelihood estimation (MLE) is a method of estimating the parameters of an assumed probability distribution, given some observed data.

>[!NOTE] MLE Definition
>$\hat{\theta}$ is the MLE if $\hat{\theta}$ maximizes $L(\theta; y_1,y_2, ... y_n)$ where $L$ is the [[likelihood function|Likelihood Function]].

>[!summary] General template for deriving MLE
>* We always use the log likelihood since it makes it much easier to derive. Think of $\ln(abc) = \ln a + \ln b + \ln c$
>* You then take the derivative, and set it to 0, since you want to maximize it.

**We define**

* $L(\theta$) as the likelihood
* $l(\theta)$ as the log likelihood
